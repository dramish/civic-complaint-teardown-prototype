# Survey Questions (Google Form)

**Status: planned, not yet run.**

## Consent line (top of the form)

"This is a short survey for a personal product-research project. Your answers are anonymous. Results may be shared in aggregate, and individual answers may be shared anonymously in a public portfolio. Do not include your name, phone number or email in any answer."

## Screener

1. Have you filed a civic complaint (garbage, sewage, potholes, streetlights, etc.) in the last 2 years? (Yes / No)  *End the survey on No.*
2. Which channel did you use? (Swachhata app / Another government app / Municipal helpline / Social media / Other)
3. Which city or town? (free text; optional)

## Experience

4. What was the complaint about? (short free text)
5. What status did you eventually see? (Resolved / Rejected / Still open / Never heard back)
6. If it showed "Resolved": was the problem actually fixed? (Yes, fully / Partly / No / Not sure)
7. If it was not fixed, what did you do next? (Reopened / Filed again / Contacted someone / Gave up / Other)
8. If you tried to reopen or complain again, what got in the way? (free text)
9. Did you ever feel your complaint closed without anyone checking? Describe what happened. (free text)

## Reaction to the proposed idea

*Show a one-line description: "Before a complaint is marked Resolved, you would get a message with the proof photo and could confirm or say it is not fixed."*

10. Would you use this? (Definitely / Probably / Not sure / Probably not / No)
11. How would you prefer to receive it? (WhatsApp / SMS / App notification / Phone call / Other)
12. How long would you realistically take to respond? (A few hours / 1 day / 2 days / More)
13. What would make you ignore it? (free text)

## Follow-up

14. Would you be open to a 10-minute call? (Yes / No). If yes, leave a way to contact you. *Contact details are kept private and never committed to this repo.*
